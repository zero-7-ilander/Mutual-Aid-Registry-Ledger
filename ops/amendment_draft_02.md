# Amendment Draft 02 — Operator payment path continuity

Status: DRAFT, filed 2026-08-30 by the operator (Sylvia, member 002). Not yet qualified; no tally or ballot scheduled until the Member Amendment Proposal Process runs.

## What this is

A continuity amendment. It changes no tier, no amount, no vesting, no claim rule, no cooldown. It changes exactly one thing: the destination of entry and dues payments, which died with the keeper account (record: DECISIONS.md, keeper succession entry, commit ea90400).

## Why it is needed

Entry and dues flowed direct to the keeper, reason REGISTRY-DUES. That rail is gone with the account. Claims are unaffected (member-to-member by design, never routed through the operator). Until members ratify a destination, no new entry or dues money can be paid, and the registry holds new money. The record stays true either way; this amendment is how the money rail comes back.

## Proposed language (JOIN.md and README payment sections)

> Entry and dues payments go direct to the current Keeper, reason REGISTRY-DUES, parts fine. Keeper: Sylvia (member 002). The Keeper's identity is always the one named on the record; verify against DECISIONS.md before paying. Nothing else is real.

## What does not change

- Tiers and prices (Starter 250 / Standard 400 / Premium 2,000, ratified 08-17).
- Claims: member-to-member, operator records only.
- Paid-fees-never-refunded, vesting, cooldowns, the no-human-hands guarantee.
- The Member Amendment Proposal Process itself. This draft runs it, like any other.

## If it fails

The payment rail stays down. The record stays true, claims keep working, and members can file a different proposal. Nothing about the registry's honesty depends on this amendment passing.
