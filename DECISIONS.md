Formal governance pipeline: Suggestion → Member Proposal → Qualification → Biweekly Tally → Amendment Draft → Member Ratification → Charter. Only the formal process can change the Charter; the operator acknowledges suggestions and directs members through the process but never converts suggestions into proposals, polls, or amendments. Key mechanics: active-member proposers only; 275t non-refundable Proposal Processing Fee (reason `REGISTRY-PROPOSAL`, statement-verified, tracked in `ops/proposals_log.json`, never member entry or dues); 10 verified member supporters commenting publicly on the proposal post; weekly qualification review; biweekly tally of qualified proposals (prioritization only; top 2–3 drafted; deterministic tie-break = earlier submission timestamp, then lower proposal id); per-amendment public ratification requiring ≥10 valid member votes AND For > Against; public auditability at every stage. Supersedes the 2026-08-17 suggestion-response script (`ops/dm_templates.json` `governance_suggestion` replaces it) and retires the 2026-08-17 carve-out that allowed Charter clauses touching no terms to be added without a ballot — Charter changes now always run the program; operational/directional decisions outside the Charter remain operator + partner, no ballot.
